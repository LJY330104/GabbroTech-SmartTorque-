03 / Why it matters

The smallest delay can shape the whole task.

EVA work combines heavy gloves, limited time and unforgiving operating conditions. SmartTorque addresses practical sources of lost motion.

01

Glove-limited input

Voice control of torque and attachment locks, together with a readable LCD, reduces reliance on detailed button sequences.

02

Repeated tool changes

Common coupling lets operators move between compatible operations with fewer separate devices.

03

Loose hardware risk

Tethering and locked attachments support deliberate handling in microgravity.

04

Variable task demands

Programmable limits make rotational tasks easier to define and repeat.

04 / Technology highlights

Control from command to motion.

The product concept combines an onboard voice-recognition module, LCD feedback and an Arduino-based control board with the mechanical tool platform.

Configurable operationPreset torque, rotational speed and turn count; stop or alert when a defined condition is reached.
Integrated feedbackThe LCD presents device parameters and key operating information where the user can check it.
Environment-focused designStructural, material and electronic protection is being designed for vacuum, temperature variation, radiation and electromagnetic interference.

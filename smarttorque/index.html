01 / What it is

A powered platform, not a single-purpose tool.

SmartTorque combines a handheld powered tool platform with embedded control, interchangeable work heads and a workflow designed for gloved use.

Product proposition

Set the operation. Select the head. Keep attention on the task.

Design intent

Built for adaptable work

The same body supports cutting, gripping and controlled nut driving through matched quick-change interfaces.

View task heads
02 / Key features

Intelligence where it helps most.

Each feature is intended to reduce a specific operating burden: finding a setting, changing a task, reading system state or retaining hardware.

01 / Command

Voice-controlled torque and head lock

Adjust torque and open or close the attachment lock by voice. Unlock the head, replace it manually, then lock it in place.

02 / Precision

Programmable motion limits

Set torque, rotational speed and turn count. At the defined threshold, the system is designed to stop motion or alert the operator.

03 / Awareness

At-a-glance LCD feedback

The LCD directly displays device parameters and working status, helping the operator confirm settings before each action.

04 / Retention

Secure by construction

The printed collar, curved ring and head-locking structure are designed to reduce tool-loss risk during handling and task changes.

04 / How it works

A clearer sequence for complex work.

The operator stays in control while SmartTorque handles repeatable setup and monitored execution.

01

Fit the head

Connect a compatible attachment and confirm the positive lock.

02

Set torque

Set the required torque by voice or manually. Unlock the attachment for a manual head change, then close the lock after fitting the next head.

03

Review settings

Check torque, speed and turn count on the LCD before the action.

04

Execute with feedback

Work to the programmed limit, with a stop or alert at the selected condition.

05 / Why SmartTorque

Designed around the realities of EVA.

The platform concept responds to the constraints of gloved handling, limited task time and hardware accountability in microgravity.

01

Fewer manual inputs

Voice adjustment of torque and head-lock control reduces fine manual input.

02

Faster task transitions

One body accepts multiple attachments through a common coupling.

03

Repeatable execution

Programmed parameters support consistent rotational operations.

04

Retention built in

Locking heads and a tether point support hardware control.

06 / System architecture

The components behind the control.

The current concept combines accessible operator interfaces with an Arduino-based controller, a powered drive and a modular mechanical output.

Voice-recognition module

Receives spoken instructions to adjust torque and open or close the attachment lock.

LCD screen

Presents operating parameters and system feedback.

Arduino-based main board

Coordinates inputs, stored settings, sensing logic and motor-control commands in the prototype architecture.

Encoder and feedback shaft

The encoder, shaft and associated gears provide a mechanical path for rotational feedback in the prototype.

Powered drive and battery

Supplies controlled motion and portable energy for repeated operations.

Quick-change coupling and retention

Connects task heads with a positive lock and supports tethering of the body.

Environment-led design

Protection designed into structure, materials and electronics.

Development priorities include operation in vacuum, temperature variation, radiation exposure and electromagnetic interference. Qualification levels and test results will be published when verified.

Arduino refers to the prototype control architecture. Flight electronics and environmental qualification require separate engineering validation.
